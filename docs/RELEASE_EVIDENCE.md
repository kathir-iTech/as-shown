Recorded examples, editor, source inspection, decision helpers and local package exchange do not need a key. Live operation requires the server-side configuration documented in the README. Do not enable live generation for ordinary CI. Financial safety depends on prepaid-only account settings as well as app caps; it is not a permanent unlimited-free-service guarantee.

## Honest limits and open observations

Three fictional garments and two synthetic shopper framings do not establish real-world usefulness, broad body/lighting/garment accuracy, reduced returns, conversion lift, fit or authenticity. No independent buyer/seller study has been performed. Automated axe/keyboard/mobile-viewport checks are not a complete screen-reader audit or physical-device test. A real 48-hour saved-artifact observation has not yet elapsed for this release; no clock acceleration or timestamp substitution is used. The original earlier artifact baseline is retained.

Redis and YouCam do not share an atomic transaction. A provider-accepted task with a lost response can remain unresolved; the product stops rather than silently creating another task. Hosted quotas and provider credits are finite; offline records remain usable when external capabilities pause. Exact-value secret scans and a zero-vulnerability dependency report are useful checks, not proof of complete security.
