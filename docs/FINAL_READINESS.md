## Honest limits — not fabricated completion claims

This is not a production-scale certification, independent user study, comprehensive screen-reader/physical-device assessment, broad load/security audit, fit/sizing guarantee, authenticity service, or defect detector. Three synthetic garment scenarios do not establish accuracy across real people, body types, lighting or garments, nor measured reductions in returns.

Seller links become inaccessible at 24 hours. Daily cleanup can retain the physical hosted file for approximately 48 hours plus scheduling delay; revocation explicitly confirms deletion or reports it pending. Previous downloads cannot be recalled. Provider-held images follow the provider's own policy; clearing app-local data cannot erase them.

The elapsed-time observation proves only that one unchanged saved artifact reopened at that observed time. Digests establish internal file agreement, not seller truth, trusted timestamps or physical condition. Exact-value secret scans and passing tests do not prove complete security. No guaranteed win, novelty percentage or winning probability is asserted.
